GOOD TO KNOW

Questions? We've
got you.

A few quick answers before you get in touch.

How do I book a service?

Send an enquiry through the form or WhatsApp once the official number is connected. Our team will confirm service details, coverage, availability and price before accepting the booking.

Which areas do you cover?

Apnora24 is being set up for Delhi NCR. Service availability is checked by locality and schedule, so please share your PIN code or area with your enquiry.

Are the prices inclusive of GST?

The hourly rates shown are marked as plus GST. For fixed packages, applicable GST and any travel charges will be clarified in the final quote before confirmation.

Does Apnora24 provide nursing or medical treatment?

No. The services described on this website are assistance services, not diagnosis, nursing procedures, medical treatment or ambulance services. Any regulated clinical service would require a separately approved and appropriately qualified service model.

Can I request urgent assistance?

You can enquire about urgent non-medical assistance. It is subject to actual team and provider availability and is not an emergency medical response service. For emergencies in India, call 112.

What happens if I need to cancel?

The proposed cancellation schedule is shown in the pricing section. The applicable policy and amount will be shared during confirmation. Please verify the final approved policy before placing a booking.
